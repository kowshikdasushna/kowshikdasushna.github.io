About

Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), interested to work on Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Natural Language Processing and Computer Vision.

For my undergraduate thesis, I proposed a training-free alternative to reinforcement-learning-based RAG that integrates fuzzy-match retrieval with task-specific decoding to improve large-language-model performance without additional training. The work is supervised by Md. Rashadur Rahman, Assistant Professor, Department of CSE, CUET.

Alongside my thesis, I have co-authored peer-reviewed work spanning student-attentiveness detection, earthquake-magnitude prediction, behavioural customer segmentation etc.

I am seeking funded graduate research opportunities in Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, NLP and Computer Vision.

Feel free to email me (kowshikdas50 at gmail dot com) if you're interested in collaboration or discussing research!
